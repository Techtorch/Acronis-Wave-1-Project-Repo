# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| L2C | Lead-to-Cash: the full flow from lead to opportunity, quote, order, invoice and renewal. |
| Wave 1 / Wave 1A | First delivery wave of the Acronis programme. [INSERT what Wave 1A covers versus later waves] |
| Torchbearers | TechTorch's roster of 11 delivery-role agents (Spark, Kindle, Ember, Flare, Lantern, Forge, Crucible, Ignition, Hearth, Afterglow, Watchtower), from the Salesforce-Delivery-Beacons repo. |
| Recommendation chain | Client need -> TechTorch offering -> Business outcome -> Proof point. |

| RAID | Risks, Assumptions, Issues and Dependencies log. |
| PE | Private equity owner of the client. |
