## What it commits to
- Seven workstreams plus SKO launch and program governance, over a one-week mobilisation and 13 working weeks (Oct 5, 2026 to Jan 15, 2027). Scope, owners and out-of-scope list are in `context/engagement-overview.md`.
- Four Stages: Design and Establish Control (Wk 1 - 6), Build and Pilot (Wk 4 - 10), Confirm and Go / No-Go (Wk 11), SKO Launch and Adoption (Wk 12 - 13).
- Stage Gates: requirements approval (Wk 3), design sign-off (Wk 5), Build Complete (Dec 11), Pilot Week review (Dec 18), first Go / No-Go (Dec 24), second Go / No-Go (Jan 1, only if needed), Wave 1 close (Jan 15).
- Fixed capacity commercial model; fees not split by workstream or between core and CDW pilot. Bi-weekly invoicing, Net 30. Capacity changes with two weeks' notice.

## Acronis responsibilities (assumptions)
- Governance confirmed before Week 1, with delivery outcomes in owners' objectives.
- Platform team names builders from Week 1.
- Read-only prod, full metadata, sandboxes, deployment path and data extracts by end of mobilisation. Access delays move dependent milestones day for day.
- Requirements approved by end of Week 3; decisions within two business days.
- Owning sellers available to validate the 620 reconciliations in Stage 1.
- Pilot Sellers named by Week 7 and protected Dec 14 - 18.
- Release management approves Pilot Week and Jan 1 deployments, including any year-end freeze exception; named testers Dec 14 - 24 and decision-makers for Go / No-Go.
- SKO dates, slots and presenters confirmed by Week 8. Training every seller by Jan 15 is an Acronis-owned outcome.
- Acronis secures CDW participation and data agreements.
